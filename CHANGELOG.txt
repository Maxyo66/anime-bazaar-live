ANIME BAZAAR — CHANGELOG FOR PLAYERS
Updates #53–#98 | November 17, 2026

LATEST UPDATES (#93–#98)
- 54 new hero items were added for nine heroes, with 18 new hidden item-pair synergies.
- The item audit made effects within each hero pool more distinct and rebalanced 23 outliers. Twelve repetitive events were redesigned with different choices and mechanics.
- Owned monster loot now shows its sell price. Five attack animations were refined, building on the unique animation for every card.

EARLIER UPDATES (#87–#92)
- Optional anonymous balancing stats: when enabled, completed non-Daily runs share a gameplay summary (hero, difficulty, result, build, fights and hidden pairs). No player name or profile ID is included. The Worker keeps only a hashed IP for its 40-per-day limit; run summaries expire after 180 days. Toggle it in Settings > Privacy; it is on by default. View aggregate totals at https://maxyo66.github.io/anime-bazaar-live/stats.html.
- Every player card now has its own attack animation. Optional effect textures can be turned on or off in Settings.
- Each hour now has one market, one event, one fight and a free pick. Shops scale to higher tiers, the Forge upgrades items for gold, and free item upgrades are available at levels 10, 15 and 20.
- 64 new events include a story event for every hero, item-tag choices and four new quests.
- Day 9 offers a four-step Expedition for a Legendary relic. Elites on days 3–8 can drop a Gate Key; losing in the Expedition ends it without a prestige penalty.

BUFFS & REWARDS
- 18 hero kits were rebuilt or rebalanced, with new passives, item effects and team-up synergies. Gold Trial skills now awaken their hero’s passive without a use cap, and Trial rewards were balanced against Ultimates.
- Monster and elite rewards now offer three different items, including one the enemy used. A Trial win still gives a normal reward if you missed its bonus objective.
- Winning a Calamity guarantees its exclusive item and grants double gold and XP. Each Calamity also has its own animated arena.
- Examples of hero upgrades: Deku’s Full Cowl builds Weapon damage; Levi’s blades and ODM Gear reward critical hits; Shinra and allies gain stronger Heat and team synergies.

NERFS & CHALLENGE
- Gear Fourth now applies a 3-second Slow after Luffy’s burst. Some top-end Ultimates were tuned down, and boss fights are 5% shorter to balance coordinated enemy combos.
- Bosses can no longer be skipped. Losing to a boss costs half the rival loss and can end the run.
- Monsters and bosses have coordinated item combos. Enemy descriptions no longer give away explicit counters.

RIVALS & OTHER CHANGES
- Players now share a rival pool without exchanging invite codes. While online, each finished day can add your board; rivals are matched by day and difficulty, and you will not face your own boards.
- Conqueror’s Haki freezes the enemy’s two largest items for 6 seconds at the start of a fight.
- Thirteen attack animations were redrawn or refined to better match the anime. Fights at 2× speed and faster have calmer effects.
- Friend invites also update boards automatically after you connect. Fusion asks for confirmation, item tooltips stay out of the way while dragging, and the game shows your run’s wins and losses more clearly.

Have fun, and thanks for playing!
